    Ask us on a call
    Does your team really speak Dutch?

    Your account manager does — every client talks to a Dutch contact. Our agents in Shenzhen work in Chinese and English, trained by us on the Clearcost way of working, so instructions arrive at the warehouse exactly as you meant them. Non-Dutch clients are served in English.

    How do you charge?

    Per order: product price plus shipping per line and weight, plus a small handling fee. No subscription, no setup fee, no contract.

    Do I need to buy stock upfront?

    No. Most products ship per order. For fast-moving winners we advise a small pre-stock so orders leave within 24 hours.

    What is the Clearcost backend and what does it cost?

    Our own software for orders, stock, tracking, profit, media buying and AI-assisted customer support. Every client gets access; from [X] orders a month it's included for free.

    What about VAT and import duties in the EU?

    EU parcels ship via IOSS, so VAT is settled upfront and your customer never gets a surprise bill from the courier.

    Can I use my own branding?

    Yes — logo mailers, boxes, cards and inserts, from your first order. Packaging is quoted per item so you know the exact cost.

    How do refunds and lost parcels work?

    Lost or damaged parcels are reshipped or refunded by us. Quality issues found in QC never ship — the factory covers them.

    How fast can I switch from my current agent?

    Usually within a week: sourcing and quotes in 48 hours, store connection and product mapping in a few days. We can run in parallel with your current agent.
